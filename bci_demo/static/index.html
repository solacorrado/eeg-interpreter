Before you start

Flashing warning: the arrows flicker 12–15 times a second. Don't use this if you or anyone watching has photosensitive epilepsy.

How it works: stare at a flickering arrow to press it.
PDF: clench twice quickly to turn the arrows on/off; hold a clench ~1 s to zoom in/out.
Audio: clench twice quickly to play/pause; clench once to go back 15 s.
Video: clench to play/pause.

Keyboard backup: ↑↓←→ press an arrow. J is your jaw: tap = clench, tap twice = double clench, hold 1 s = held clench. You can also click an arrow. Use the Flicker slider to make the arrows gentler.

Fake headset mode: hold Shift as well to send fake brain signals through the real detector instead (Shift+arrow = a fake look, takes about a second; Shift+J = a fake clench, as long as you hold it).
